Graduate-Ready Human Skills

Communication, Resilience and Self-Leadership for the Future Workplace

A portfolio-based textbook/workbook for universities, colleges, TAFE providers, residential colleges and student support programs.

Preview sample chapter View package inclusions

Who it is for

Employability teams, student services, careers units, work-integrated learning programs, residential colleges, tutors and facilitators supporting students into placements, internships and graduate roles.

What students build

Students create a professional profile, evidence map, communication templates, team charter, pressure plan, self-management log, ethical judgement statement and graduate readiness action plan.

Positioning

Designed to complement employability learning, orientation, student leadership, transition support and work-integrated learning. It is not an official syllabus unless adopted by an institution.

Key outcomes

Communication Resilience Self-leadership Teamwork Initiative Ethical judgement

Adoption pathway

  1. Review sample chapter and institutional adoption pack.
  2. Map chapters to local graduate attributes and employability outcomes.
  3. Insert local wellbeing, placement, academic integrity and AI-use policy references.
  4. Pilot a 45-90 minute session or two-module sequence.
  5. Use feedback to finalise the commercial release package.
