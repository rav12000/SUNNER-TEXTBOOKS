# Graduate-Ready Human Skills Institutional Adoption Pack

## One-paragraph adoption summary
**Graduate-Ready Human Skills** is a portfolio-based employability textbook designed to complement orientation, student leadership, transition, work-integrated learning and graduate readiness programs. It helps students develop and evidence communication, resilience, self-leadership, teamwork, initiative and ethical judgement through eight structured chapters, practical worksheets, portfolio tasks and assessment-ready artefacts.

## Suggested adoption pathways
| Pathway | Best fit | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation workshop | First-year or transition programs | 45-90 minute introductory session plus one portfolio task |
| Employability module | Career development or professional skills course | Eight weekly classes using one chapter per week |
| Placement preparation | Work-integrated learning programs | Chapters 3, 4, 6 and 7 plus policy-specific additions |
| Student leadership | Peer mentors, residential colleges, clubs | Chapters 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 with leadership reflection |
| Self-guided resource | Careers portal or digital shop | Downloadable textbook plus portfolio template |

## Risk and compliance review points
- Confirm the resource is described as complementary, not officially accredited unless formally approved.
- Add local student wellbeing, crisis and counselling pathways.
- Verify academic integrity, AI use, placement confidentiality and professional conduct requirements.
- Review discipline-specific examples for accuracy.
- Ensure accessibility compliance for digital and print versions.
- Check copyright and permissions before commercial distribution.

## Buyer-facing outcomes
Students who complete the program should leave with:
- a professional profile;
- a human-skills evidence map;
- communication templates;
- a team charter or collaboration artefact;
- a personal pressure plan;
- a self-management log;
- an ethical judgement statement; and
- a graduate readiness action plan.

## Pilot evaluation questions
| Stakeholder | Questions |
|---|---|
| Students | Which activity felt most useful? Which instruction was unclear? What portfolio output will you use again? |
| Facilitators | Which sessions fit the timing? Where did students need more scaffolding? What local examples should be added? |
