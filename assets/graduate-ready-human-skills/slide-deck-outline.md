# Graduate-Ready Human Skills Slide Deck Outline

## Deck purpose
A designer-ready slide plan for a 60-90 minute introductory workshop or orientation session. Slides should be visually clean, low text, and activity-led.

| Slide | Title | On-slide text | Speaker notes | Activity |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Graduate-Ready Human Skills | Communication. Resilience. Self-leadership. | Welcome students and explain this is a practical employability session. | None |
| 2 | Why this matters | Knowledge is applied through people. | Employers and placements notice behaviours: clarity, reliability, collaboration, judgement. | Pair share: where do human skills show up? |
| 3 | Program boundary | Practical learning, not therapy or crisis support. | Name support pathways and privacy expectations. | Students note one support service. |
| 4 | The portfolio mindset | Do not just claim skills. Evidence them. | Introduce portfolio as the through-line. | Quick poll: what evidence do you already have? |
| 5 | Human skills map | Communicate. Collaborate. Adapt. Lead self. Decide ethically. | Define each in observable behaviour. | Self-rate one strength and one growth area. |
| 6 | Scenario: unclear task | What would you do next? | Present a placement/internship task with unclear expectations. | Small groups draft response. |
| 7 | Communication tool | Context. Current status. Specific issue. Request. | Teach CCSR for updates and clarification. | Rewrite a vague message. |
| 8 | Professional identity | Values + strengths + growth. | Professional identity is not a costume. | Write one profile sentence. |
| 9 | Team trust | Clarity before pressure. | Introduce team charters and repair agreements. | Identify one team norm that prevents conflict. |
| 10 | Resilience under pressure | Support. Flexibility. Recovery. Action. | Resilience includes help-seeking and boundaries. | Private pressure-plan prompt. |
| 11 | Initiative and ownership | Notice. Check. Act. Update. | Initiative should be appropriate to role and context. | Prioritise a mini task list. |
| 12 | Ethical judgement | Pause before you proceed. | Introduce PAUSE: Pause, Assess, Understand, Seek, Execute. | Vote on first step in ethical scenario. |
| 13 | Your portfolio outputs | Profile, evidence map, communication sample, team artefact, pressure plan, log, ethics statement. | Show how the textbook builds a portfolio over time. | Students choose first artefact. |
| 14 | Next 7 days | One behaviour. One artefact. One support. | Convert motivation into action. | Complete commitment card. |
| 15 | Close | Graduate-ready means evidence-ready. | Thank students and point to next module/supports. | Exit ticket. |

## Visual direction
- Use warm professional colours: deep navy, warm sand, muted green and white space.
- Use icons sparingly for communication, teamwork, resilience, initiative and ethics.
- Prefer short statements, tables and scenario cards over dense paragraphs.
- Include accessibility-friendly contrast and large font sizes.
