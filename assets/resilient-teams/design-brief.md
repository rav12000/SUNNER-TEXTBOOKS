# Resilient Teams Design Brief

## Product identity

**Title:** Resilient Teams

**Subtitle:** A Practical Training Manual for Adaptability, Grit and Trust

**Format:** Participant manual plus facilitator and implementation assets

**Primary buyers:** HR, L&D, people and culture teams, managers, frontline leaders, small business owners and community organisation leaders.

## Design intent

The manual should feel professional, grounded and commercially credible. It should not look like a school workbook or a clinical wellbeing resource. The visual language should support workplace training: clear sections, generous writing space, readable tables, strong activity callouts and practical templates that can be photocopied or converted into fillable PDFs.

## Recommended visual system

- **Tone:** calm, practical, warm, trustworthy and modern.
- **Palette:** deep teal, slate blue, warm grey, off-white and one high-contrast accent colour.
- **Typography:** accessible sans serif for headings and body text, with a highly readable table style.
- **Icon style:** simple line icons for discussion, reflection, team exercise, manager note, application challenge and support pathway.
- **Page architecture:** module opener, scenario panel, concept section, exercise table, commitment box and application challenge.

## Accessibility requirements

- Use semantic heading hierarchy.
- Maintain strong colour contrast for text and table borders.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
- Keep body text at a minimum 11 pt in print layouts.
- Provide large writing spaces for worksheets.
- Ensure digital PDFs are tagged and navigable.

## Production package checklist

- Cover and title page.
- Copyright and professional disclaimer page.
- Contents page.
- Eight module openers.
- Diagnostic and evaluation forms.
- Team charter templates.
- Manager implementation roadmap.
- Final publication checklist.

## Page-count target

The manuscript includes a 100+ page layout map estimating approximately 126 designed pages. Use generous worksheet spacing, module dividers, scenario panels and reflection pages to preserve participant usability rather than compressing the resource into a dense report.

## Export recommendations

- Print PDF for participant manuals.
- Fillable PDF for worksheets and evaluation forms.
- Slide-compatible activity extracts for facilitator delivery.
- Low-resolution preview PDF for sales conversations.
