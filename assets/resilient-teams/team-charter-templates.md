## 3. Support behaviours

We will make it easier to ask for help by:

- Asking “What support would make this workable?” during pressure points.
- Naming blockers early.
- Sharing context before assuming intent.
- Directing people to appropriate workplace and professional supports when needed.

## 5. Conflict repair process

When tension affects work, we will:

1. Return to the shared standard.
2. Name the situation and impact respectfully.
3. Own our contribution where relevant.
4. Agree the next behaviour or decision pathway.
5. Use formal HR, safety or grievance pathways where informal repair is not suitable.
