# Resilient Teams Editorial QA Checklist

## Positioning

- [ ] Resource is positioned as workplace training and development.
- [ ] No claim is made that the manual is therapy, clinical treatment, legal advice or HR compliance advice.
- [ ] Commercial audience language is used instead of school language.

## Content quality

- [ ] Each module includes purpose, learning outcomes, scenario, concept, activity, exercise, worksheet, facilitator note, commitments and application challenge.
- [ ] Examples cover office, frontline, small business, hybrid, leadership and community contexts.
- [ ] Australian English spelling and tone are consistent.

## Safeguarding and boundaries

- [ ] Support statement references workplace channels, EAP, HR, managers, representatives and professional care.
- [ ] Facilitator notes avoid inviting clinical disclosure.
- [ ] Conflict and HR concerns are directed to appropriate formal pathways.

## Production readiness

- [ ] Tables are design-ready and accessible.
- [ ] Templates can be extracted into fillable worksheets.
- [ ] Facilitator guide aligns with participant manual.
- [ ] Evaluation form includes pre/post measures and qualitative feedback.
