# Resilient Teams Publication Roadmap

## Stage 1: Editorial consolidation

- Confirm the manual title, subtitle and commercial positioning.
- Review all modules for consistency of structure and voice.
- Check Australian English, plain-language clarity and workplace relevance.
- Remove any school-focused language or unsupported claims.

## Stage 2: Professional boundary review

- Review therapy, legal, HR and compliance disclaimers.
- Confirm support pathway language for the intended buyer context.
- Ensure psychological safety language is framed as practical meeting and leadership behaviours, not clinical assurance.
- Confirm conflict content appropriately directs serious concerns to formal pathways.

## Stage 3: Design preparation

- Apply the design brief to the participant manual and extracted worksheets.
- Convert templates into spacious workbook pages.
- Create print PDF, digital PDF and fillable worksheet versions.
- Prepare a sample chapter PDF for sales and pilot conversations.

## Stage 4: Pilot delivery

- Pilot as one half-day workshop and one full-day workshop.
- Collect pre/post evaluations and facilitator notes.
- Ask managers which tools were used after two weeks.
- Revise timing, examples and instructions based on observed delivery.

## Stage 5: Commercial package

- Finalise participant manual.
- Finalise facilitator guide and slide outline.
- Finalise charter templates and evaluation forms.
- Finalise buyer-facing product page and marketing copy.
- Prepare licence language for organisation-wide use.

## Stage 6: Launch readiness

- Upload sales page and sample chapter.
- Prepare launch email, LinkedIn post and discovery call talking points.
- Create a simple implementation offer: half-day workshop, full-day workshop and 90-day manager follow-up option.
