# Graduate-Ready Human Skills Marketing Copy

## Short product description
**Graduate-Ready Human Skills** is a practical, portfolio-based textbook for university, college and TAFE students preparing for internships, placements, graduate roles and early-career responsibility. It helps learners develop and evidence communication, resilience, self-leadership, teamwork, initiative and ethical judgement.

## Long product description
Technical knowledge helps students enter a field, but human skills help them contribute once work becomes real. **Graduate-Ready Human Skills: Communication, Resilience and Self-Leadership for the Future Workplace** gives students a structured way to turn study, work, volunteering and life experience into credible employability evidence.

Across eight applied chapters, students build a professional profile, human-skills evidence map, communication templates, team charter, pressure plan, self-management log, ethical judgement statement and final graduate readiness action plan. The resource is designed to complement orientation, work-integrated learning, student leadership, employability and transition programs, subject to local institutional review and adoption.

## Key selling points
- Portfolio-based structure with practical outputs in every chapter.
- Designed for universities, colleges, TAFE, residential colleges and employability programs.
- Includes facilitator guide, slide deck outline, worksheet pack and institutional adoption tools.
- Uses practical, inclusive and trauma-aware language.
- Supports communication, collaboration, resilience, initiative, professional identity and ethical judgement.
- Avoids overclaiming accreditation and includes clear institutional mapping placeholders.

## Buyer-facing outcomes
Students leave with:
1. a professional profile;
2. a human-skills evidence map;
3. professional communication templates;
4. a team charter or collaboration artefact;
5. a personal pressure plan;
6. a self-management log;
7. an ethical judgement statement; and
8. a graduate readiness action plan.

## Back cover draft
Graduate readiness is more than a qualification. It is the ability to communicate clearly, work with others, recover from setbacks, manage yourself, make values-led decisions and show evidence of your growth.

**Graduate-Ready Human Skills** is a practical textbook for students preparing for internships, placements, team projects, graduate roles and early-career work. Through eight structured chapters, learners build a portfolio of real artefacts that can support interviews, placement conversations, mentoring and professional development.

Grounded, reflective and commercially practical, this resource helps students move from vague confidence to credible evidence: what they can do, how they know and how they are continuing to grow.
