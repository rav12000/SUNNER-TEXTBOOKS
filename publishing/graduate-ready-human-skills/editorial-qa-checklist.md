# Graduate-Ready Human Skills Editorial QA Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist moves the product from manuscript and production assets into editorial QA, institutional review and publication readiness. It should be completed by the author/editor, curriculum reviewer, designer, accessibility reviewer and legal/compliance reviewer before commercial release.

## Review roles
| Role | Primary responsibility | Required output |
|---|---|---|
| Author/editor | Voice, accuracy, flow, examples and commercial polish | Clean manuscript and tracked editorial decisions |
| Curriculum/employability reviewer | Fit with local graduate attributes and employability outcomes | Completed mapping notes and adoption recommendations |
| Wellbeing/safeguarding reviewer | Boundary language, referral prompts and student safety | Approved support language and escalation notes |
| Accessibility reviewer | Inclusive design, readability and digital access | Accessibility report and remediation list |
| Legal/compliance reviewer | Copyright, claims, policy language and commercial risk | Approval notes or required amendments |
| Designer | Layout, worksheets, slide treatment and exports | Print-ready and digital files |

## Manuscript QA
- [ ] Chapter titles match the table of contents and slide deck references.
- [ ] All eight chapters include learning outcomes, scenario, concept, activity, portfolio task, assessment option and facilitator note.
- [ ] Repeated chapter structures feel consistent but not overly mechanical.
- [ ] Australian English spelling and tone are consistent.
- [ ] Examples are inclusive and not limited to one discipline, culture or employment pathway.
- [ ] Claims remain practical and evidence-informed without overpromising outcomes.
- [ ] All placeholders are either completed or intentionally retained for institutional customisation.

## Wellbeing and safeguarding QA
- [ ] The resource clearly states it is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis intervention or medical treatment.
- [ ] Reflection activities do not require disclosure of trauma, distress, personal health or unsafe experiences.
- [ ] Resilience language includes boundaries, rest, support and help-seeking.
- [ ] Facilitator scripts explain what to do when a student appears distressed.
- [ ] Local student support, counselling, crisis and emergency pathways are inserted before delivery.
- [ ] Any institution-specific duty-of-care requirements are reviewed.

## Employability and curriculum QA
- [ ] The manuscript uses “designed to complement” rather than claiming official accreditation.
- [ ] Graduate attribute mapping is completed for the adopting institution.
- [ ] Work-integrated learning references are checked against current policy.
- [ ] Academic integrity and AI-use references are checked against current policy.
- [ ] Assessment tasks are feasible within the intended course workload.
- [ ] Portfolio artefacts align with employability outcomes and can be assessed consistently.

## Accessibility QA
- [ ] Heading hierarchy is logical.
- [ ] Tables have header rows and are not used for complex layout where avoidable.
- [ ] Colour is not the only way information is communicated.
- [ ] Text contrast passes accessibility review.
- [ ] Fonts are readable in print and digital formats.
- [ ] Worksheets provide adequate writing space.
- [ ] PDF exports are tagged where required.
- [ ] Slide deck is readable from the back of a room and on small screens.

## Commercial QA
- [ ] Product name, subtitle and positioning are consistent across manuscript, README, slide deck and adoption pack.
- [ ] Buyer-facing copy clearly states audience, outcomes and use cases.
- [ ] Licensing assumptions for photocopying, institutional use and facilitator reproduction are clarified.
- [ ] Sample chapter and preview assets do not include restricted facilitator-only content.
- [ ] File naming and version numbers are consistent.
- [ ] Final package includes manuscript, facilitator guide, slide deck, worksheet pack, adoption pack and marketing copy.
